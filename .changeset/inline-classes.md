`buttonClassName`, `badgeClassName`, `chipClassName`, `tabsClassName`, `tabListClassName`, `tabClassName` and `tabPanelClassName` are no longer exported. To style another element like a component, render the component itself (`Button`, `Badge` and `Chip` take `as` or `href`), or use your own classes; the components look and behave as before.
